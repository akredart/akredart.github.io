How to make a bunny loft

By Ariel

This is a step by step website!

Step One

You will need a piece of plywood, two two by four eight foot planks, cute fencing, small wooden dowels, fabric, fabric glue, screws and a hinge.

Step Two

First you will want to take your piece of plywood and cut it to your perfered size, I suggest three by four feet.

Step Three

Now you want to build your frame that will attach underneath the plywood base. Take your two by four planks and cut two four foot pieces and two two foot pieces and screw them together to make a frame.

Step Four

Once you have your frame and base you are going to pause with the wood and started your fabric placement. Place your selected fabric on the top of the base and flip it over, on the underneath side you will want to glue the edges down and trim any acesss you have left.

Step Five

Place your base with the fabric on it, on top of the frame and screw down into the frame starting with the corners and filling each side until sturdy.

Step Six

Next you are going to want to start on your four legs. Take the pther two by four plank and cut it equally three times, resulting in four two feet legs. Turn your base and frame upside down and screw the legs in one by one through the outside of the frame. Not through the top of the base!

Step Seven

You should now have a table-like struture with fabic on the base! The next step is to build the ramp. Use the other piece of plywood to decide your desired angle, once you do that you will take your fabric and wrap it around the ramp, just like you did to the base, and glue to down.

Step Eight

When the glue dries, take the dowels and seperate them along the ramp so that your bunny friend can climb up! Take nails and put two through the midway points of each dowels so they do not rotate.

Step Nine

Decide where you want your ramp to be angled and connect the hinge to the top of the ramp with screws. Lift the struture up, so that the ramp is completely vertical, and screw in the other side of the hinge. Slowly lower the struture and lift the ramp so that it stands at an angle

Step Ten

Take your fencing and install it along the border of your struture. I recommend starting at the top of the ramp and making my way around the edges, using screws to secure it.

Conclusion

Set up your struture in your bunny area and watch your bunny run up and down, underneath, and have a worry free bunny parent if they decide to chew on the wood!
